Tueste

Privacy policy

Effective November 5, 2026

In short: Tueste does not collect, send or share any data. It has no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and it does not even have permission to connect to the internet. Everything you save stays on your phone.

Who is responsible

Tueste is an Android app developed by Lluis Menendez. If you have any questions about this policy, write to lluismf88@gmail.com.

What the app stores

To work, Tueste stores the information you enter on your device:

  • Your coffee bags: roaster, origin, name, process, size, remaining grams, roast date and notes.
  • Photos of your bags that you pick from your gallery or take with the camera. The app keeps a resized copy in its internal storage.
  • Your brews: type, dose, yield, grind, time, temperature, milk, rating, notes, date and time.
  • A few of the app's own preferences.

This data is stored only in the app's private storage on your device. It is never sent to any server, ours or anyone else's. We have no access to it.

What the app does not do

  • It does not ask you to create an account or sign in.
  • It does not show ads.
  • It has no analytics, tracking or automatic crash reporting.
  • It does not use your location, your contacts or advertising identifiers.
  • It does not declare the internet permission, so it cannot send data anywhere.

Permissions and photos

Tueste does not ask for camera or storage permissions. For photos it uses the Android photo picker and the system camera app: it only receives the specific picture you choose or take, never the rest of your gallery.

Android backup

Tueste allows standard Android backup. If backup is turned on in your device settings, Android may include the app's data and photos in the backup stored in your own Google account, and may also move them to a new phone when you transfer your data. This backup is handled by your device's operating system together with Google, according to your settings and Google's terms; we cannot see or receive that data. Android limits the size of each app's backup, so not everything may be included.

You can turn backup off in Android settings (usually Settings > Google > Backup, or Settings > System > Backup, depending on the manufacturer).

Deleting your data

You are fully in control:

  • You can delete brews and bags (with their photo) from within the app.
  • You can delete everything from Android Settings > Apps > Tueste > Storage > Clear data, or by uninstalling the app.

Since we do not process your data on any server, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Children

Tueste is not directed at children under 13 and does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app changes how data is handled, we will update this page and its effective date before releasing it.
